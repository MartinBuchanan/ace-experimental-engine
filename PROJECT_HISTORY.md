# Project history

The ACE Experimental Engine began before ACE Studio exposed its expanded official CLI/MCP functionality. Early engineering therefore concentrated on controlled observation, explicit mutation authority, evidence preservation and validation around the interfaces then available.

That work established a substantial private development baseline. It remains valuable evidence about safe execution, verification boundaries and experimental design.

In September 2026, the expanded official CLI/MCP interface prompted a reassessment of the optimal architecture. The project is now evaluating how supported ACE execution can combine with autonomous experimental planning, measurement, scoring, evidence and learning.

The reassessment may lead components to be retained, adapted, replaced or retired. It does not mean earlier engineering was wasted or failed.
