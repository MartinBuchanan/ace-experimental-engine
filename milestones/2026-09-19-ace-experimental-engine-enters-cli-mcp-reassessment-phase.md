# ACE Experimental Engine enters CLI/MCP reassessment phase

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Engine version:** v1.1.0  
**Category:** architecture

## What changed

ACE Studio's expanded official CLI/MCP interface may replace or simplify parts of the original custom control layer. The project is beginning a structured capability reassessment while retaining earlier validation as useful evidence.

## Verified findings

- A validated private development baseline exists.
- The reassessment framework separates Documented, Exposed, Tested and Verified evidence states.
- Later architecture decisions will classify components as Retain, Adapt, Replace or Retire.

## Why it matters

Supported ACE execution may simplify the control layer and allow engineering effort to focus on experiment planning, measurement, evidence and learning.

## Next step

Run a structured public-facing capability census without treating documentation or exposure as verification.
